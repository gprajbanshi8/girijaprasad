01. Introduction to ICT

ICT stands for Information and Communication Technology. It includes digital tools and systems used to create, store, process and communicate information.

02. Computer Hardware

Hardware refers to the physical parts of a computer, such as the keyboard, mouse, monitor, CPU, printer and storage devices.

03. Computer Software

Software is a set of programs that tells a computer what to do. Examples include operating systems, word processors, browsers and presentation software.

04. Internet & Networking

A computer network connects devices so they can communicate and share resources. The internet is a worldwide network of interconnected networks.

05. Cyber Security

Use strong passwords, enable multi-factor authentication, keep software updated and avoid suspicious links or attachments.

06. Digital Literacy

Digital literacy means having the knowledge and skills to find, evaluate, create and communicate information using digital technologies.
